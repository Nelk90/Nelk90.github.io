Behind the games

Small studio.
Personal touch.

Kenzory is an independent, one-person game project. A space to explore simple ideas, give them a little character, and turn them into something worth spending a quiet moment with.

There is no big team behind the screen. Just a developer working on the details: how a puzzle feels, how a creature makes you smile, and what makes you want to try once more.
